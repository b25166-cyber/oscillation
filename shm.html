⚡ Energy in Simple Harmonic Motion

Understand how potential and kinetic energy continuously interchange while total energy remains conserved.

Kinetic Energy (K)

Energy associated with the motion of the block. Maximum at equilibrium, zero at extremes.

K = ½mv² = ½kA² sin²(ωt)

Potential Energy (U)

Stored elastic energy in the deformed spring. Maximum at extremes, zero at equilibrium.

U = ½kx² = ½kA² cos²(ωt)

Total Mechanical Energy (E)

In an ideal system without friction, total energy is constant at all times.

E = K + U = ½kA²

🧠 Explore the Concepts

Important Class 11 ideas behind the experiment.

What is SHM?

Simple harmonic motion is oscillatory motion in which acceleration is proportional to displacement and directed opposite to it.

a = −ω²x

Amplitude

The maximum displacement from the equilibrium position. Its SI unit is metre.

Maximum |x| = A

Time Period

The time required to complete one full oscillation.

T = 2π/ω

Frequency

The number of complete oscillations per second. Its unit is hertz.

f = 1/T

Restoring Force

For an ideal spring, the restoring force points opposite to displacement.

F = −kx

Angular Frequency

It describes how quickly the phase of the oscillation changes.

ω = √(k/m)

✏️ SHM Equations & Formulas

Connect the graphs and physics equations together.

x = A cos(ωt)

Displacement from the positive extreme.

v = −Aω sin(ωt)

Velocity is the rate of change of displacement.

a = −ω²x

Acceleration is opposite to displacement.

vmax = Aω

Maximum speed occurs at equilibrium.

amax = Aω²

Maximum acceleration occurs at the extremes.

E = ½kA²

Total energy depends on stiffness and amplitude squared.

🧮 Worked Examples

Try the question before opening the solution.

Example 1: Find the time period

A 2 kg block is attached to a spring of stiffness 50 N/m. Find T.

Show solution
T = 2π√(m/k)
T = 2π√(2/50)
Answer: T ≈ 1.26 s

Example 2: Find maximum speed

An oscillator has amplitude 0.1 m and angular frequency 4 rad/s.

Show solution
vmax = Aω = 0.1 × 4
Answer: 0.4 m/s

Example 3: Find total mechanical energy

A spring with stiffness 40 N/m has an amplitude of 0.2 m. Find the total energy.

Show solution
E = ½kA² = 0.5 × 40 × (0.2)²
E = 20 × 0.04
Answer: 0.8 J
